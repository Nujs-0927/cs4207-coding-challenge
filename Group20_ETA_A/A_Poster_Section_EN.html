Data preparation

  • Read selected Parquet columns in 100,000-row batches.
  • Derive the label from dropoff minus pickup time, in minutes.
  • Keep in-month pickups; remove non-positive or extreme duration/distance, invalid fares, and implausible observed speeds.
  • Project thresholds: 1–180 minutes, distance ≤100 miles, speed ≤100 mph. Fixed scope choices, with selection limitations.
  • Retain missing passenger count and rate code; mark missing categories explicitly. 993,400 Flex Fare trips are retained.

Leakage & reproducibility

Dropoff time, realised distance, speed, fares, tips, payment and final rate code are excluded from the pickup-time feature whitelist. Use time-based train/validation/test splits and train-only learned preprocessing. Stable row IDs and fixed-order shard aggregation preserve alignment.

Source: NYC TLC January 2026 Yellow Taxi file. Counts are ordered and non-overlapping. Actual recorded dropoff zone is used only under an intended-destination assumption. This evaluation population excludes invalid/extreme observed trips.
