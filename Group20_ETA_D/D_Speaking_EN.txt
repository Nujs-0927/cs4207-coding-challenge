Likely questions
- Why log duration? Durations are right-skewed; on the log scale one 60-minute trip does not dominate the loss. Predictions are converted back with expm1 and clipped to A's 1–180 minute range.
- Why not distance? The recorded distance is only known after the trip: leakage. All inputs are known at pickup.
- How did you tune? Small grids per model, fitted on train and chosen by validation MAE; LightGBM/XGBoost stop early on validation loss. Test was scored once, for the chosen configuration.
- Why drop day_of_month? Training covers days 1–21, test 27–31; a tree would treat every test day as day 21.
- Why is test worse than validation? Snowstorm on 25 January: test bias -4.8 min, daily MAE up to 7.3 min on 2026-01-28; it falls back to 4.2 on 2026-01-31. Every model, including the baselines, shows the same drop.
- Why are long trips under-predicted? Few long trips per zone pair, no traffic or weather input, and a log-scale loss pulls predictions toward the typical trip.
- Random Forest non-determinism? scikit-learn adds the 300 tree predictions from several threads as each finishes. Floating-point addition is not associative, so the last digits change (1e-13 min). One prediction thread fixes the order; training stays parallel and seeded.
- Is the result reproducible? Fixed seed 420720: identical fingerprints for refits and for 1 vs 2 threads, for all three models.
- Why LightGBM over XGBoost? Validation MAE 3.89 vs 3.93; chosen on validation only.
- Feature importance metric? Share of total split gain; zone-pair encoding alone ≈ 53%.
